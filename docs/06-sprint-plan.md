# 6. Sprint Plan

## 6.1 Sprint settings
- **Sprint length:** 1 week
- **Capacity:** about 12 hours per week (2 hours per day, 6 days)
- **Velocity:** unknown. Sprint 1 is an estimate and will be adjusted from
  actual results.
- **Commitment rule:** do not fill a sprint beyond estimated velocity.

## 6.2 Scrum ceremonies (solo version)

| Ceremony | Solo version |
|----------|--------------|
| Sprint planning | 20 minutes at the start of the week: pick items, write the goal |
| Daily standup | 3-line note: done, next, blocked |
| Sprint review | Short demo recording or screenshots |
| Retrospective | 10 minutes: went well / did not / one change. Saved in `docs/retros/` |

**Goal:** Architecture, database design, wireframes and tools are ready.
**Work:** Phases 2, 3 and 4 of the learning plan.
