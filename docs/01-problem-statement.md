# 1. Problem Statement

## 1.1 The Problem
Large organisations with multiple departments manage projects across scattered
tools: chat, email, spreadsheets and document drives. Project information is
fragmented, progress is hard to see, and routine work (writing meeting notes,
reports and task lists) consumes manager time. The result is missed deadlines,
duplicated work and poor cross-team visibility. University project teams face
the same problem on a smaller scale, with students and supervisors coordinating
through informal channels.

## 1.2 Product Positioning
**For** project managers, team members and academic supervisors
**who** need one clear view of tasks, deadlines and team progress,
**our product** is a web-based project management and collaboration platform
**that** centralises tasks, files and discussion, and uses AI to automate
routine work such as task creation, summaries and risk detection.
**Unlike** generic task boards where users enter and maintain everything
manually, **our product** integrates AI assistance directly into the
planning workflow and supports both enterprise teams and university projects.

## 1.3 Success Criteria
- A manager can see the status of all projects on one dashboard.
- A sprint summary can be generated in under one minute.
- Tasks can be generated from a text description.
- Every action is restricted by role (Admin, Manager, Member).
